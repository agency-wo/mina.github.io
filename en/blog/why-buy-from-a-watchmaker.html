Why Buy Your Watch from a Watchmaker

Most places that sell watches have never opened one. We have been repairing them in Durrës since 2009, and the same hands that do that work choose and inspect everything we list. Here is what that actually changes.

5 min read August 2026

Anybody can sell a watch. A phone, a photograph and a supplier is the whole of the setup, and most of the watches sold in Albania are sold by somebody who has never had the back off one. We came at it from the other direction: the bench first, the shelf second.

The Person Choosing the Stock Has Been Repairing Watches Since 2002

Our owner began as a watchmaker working for other people in 2002 and opened Iglisi Watch in Durrës in 2009. It is still a family-owned shop and the repair bench is still the main business. Alongside the watches, we have cut keys here for years, and passed the half million mark in 2020.

That history is not decoration. Somebody who repairs watches every day sees which brands come back, which crowns strip, which bracelets shed pins after a summer, and which cheap movements die quietly in the second year. A seller who never sees a watch again after the sale learns none of that, and has no reason to want to.

Every Watch Is Inspected Before It Is Listed

The same hands that repair a watch select and inspect every watch we list for sale. Case and crystal for marks, crown for a clean action through its positions, bracelet and clasp for play, and the movement running properly before anything goes on the shelf.

It is a small thing that gets said a lot and meant rarely. When we write that a watch has been checked, we mean somebody sat down with it under a light and a loupe. Everything we list is brand new, and it still gets that look.

What a Watchmaker Will Not Write Down

The most useful test of a watch seller is what they refuse to claim. Ours is written into the way this whole site is put together.

  • Sapphire only where we can prove it. Sapphire crystal is claimed on the Hislon range and one Romanson, and nowhere else, because that is where we know it.
  • No movement stated for Philippe Lauren. We have not verified one, so we do not publish one, even though it would read better if we did.
  • Chronograph only when the pushers have been tested. Plenty of watches have three subdials and no stopwatch behind them. We describe the dial on those and say nothing about what it does. That distinction is the whole of our guide to chronographs and lookalikes.
  • No water-resistance claim without a rating. If a rating is not printed and confirmed, we do not invent one.

None of that is modesty. It is what happens when the person writing the description is the person who will be handed the watch when it fails.

The Guarantee Is Honoured by the People Who Gave It

Every watch carries our own 1-year guarantee from the date of purchase, on top of any manufacturer warranty. What makes it worth something is that the workshop honouring it is the workshop that sold it. Nothing is posted abroad and nobody refers you to a form. You walk in with the watch, we open it here, and either we fix it or we replace it.

Compare that with a private seller in a direct message, who cannot give you a warranty at all and may not have an account next month. We set that out in detail in our guide to buying a watch on Instagram in Albania. Alongside it sits the plain version of the returns policy in our guide to returns, exchanges and the guarantee.

The Years After the Sale

A watch is not finished when you pay for it. A quartz watch wants a battery every couple of years, and we change one for 200 to 600 Lekë depending on the battery, while you wait. Bracelets get sized free at the counter. A crystal, a crown, a strap or a full service is quoted before we start, and we will tell you honestly when a repair is not worth the money.

All of that happens whether or not you bought the watch here, which is the point. The shop was a repair shop first and it will be one long after any particular sale.
