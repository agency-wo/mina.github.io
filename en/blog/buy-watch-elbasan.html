What a Watchmaker Knows That a Reseller Does Not

Iglisi Watch has been trading since 2009 and the bench came first. Repairs are still most of the working day: batteries, crystals, crowns, straps, bracelet links, cases opened and closed again. Watches are sold from the same counter, in the same room, by the same hands, and the counter runs from €50 to €249.

That order of events matters more than it sounds. A shop that only sells sees a watch once, on the day the money changes hands. We see the second half of a watch's life. Which movements arrive dead in year four and which are still running in year ten. Which plated cases go patchy at the lugs. Which crowns strip. Which crystals scratch until the owner stops reading the dial and starts guessing at it. A reseller ships a sealed box and has no opinion about what is inside it, because nothing ever comes back for an opinion to form around.

So when we say a watch is worth the money, that is a judgement assembled out of repairs rather than out of a supplier's catalogue photograph. The general version of this argument is set out in why buy your watch from a watchmaker. What follows is the same argument tested against the one situation that makes it harder to hold, a buyer who is not in the same city.

What That Changes for a Buyer in Elbasan

The recommendation is made by someone who has seen the failures. Ask for a watch for a teacher, for somebody on a building site, for an eighteen-year-old, and the answer is filtered through what came back to the bench and what never did. When you cannot handle the watch first, that filtering is worth more, not less.

The guarantee is honoured by the people who would do the work. Our own 1-year guarantee runs on top of any manufacturer warranty. It is not administered by a distributor in another country and it does not route through a call centre. It covers a fault in the watch. It does not cover accidental damage, water damage past the rating printed on the dial, the battery, strap wear, or a case that somebody else has already opened.

Something that goes wrong ends up on a bench, not in a postal system. A reseller's warranty claim is a parcel that leaves and comes back in some number of weeks, or does not come back. Ours ends up in front of the person who sold it to you.

Here is the part a page like this usually leaves out. Our services are in person only. For a buyer in Elbasan that is a genuine cost, and it belongs in the decision rather than in a footnote. A jeweller two streets from your house can put a battery in on a Tuesday afternoon. We cannot do that for you, and no amount of good intent about the bench changes it.

What We Do So That Stays Rare

The answer is not a slogan. It is a handful of ordinary practices that between them keep most watches off the bench for a long time.

Everything is new and everything carries a 1-year guarantee. A new quartz watch in its early years is close to the least demanding object in a household. The failures we see arriving at the counter do not really start until a watch is well past its first battery.

A battery is 200 to 600 Lek, and we change it for anyone. Whether the watch came off our counter or out of a shop in Elbasan twelve years ago, the price is the same and the job is done while the owner waits rather than kept overnight. There has never been a loyalty test attached to a battery here. The detail is in our piece on watch battery replacement.

We say plainly when a repair is not worth doing. Plenty of watches go back unrepaired, because the part costs more than the watch or the case is past saving. That is the least profitable sentence in the shop and it is also the reason people ask us a second time.

We choose stock partly on how little it asks of the owner. That is a real filter rather than a phrase, and it is the whole reason a sealed digital Casio on a long-life battery is on the counter at all.

What You Are Choosing Between in Elbasan

Elbasan has jewellers and accessory counters that carry watches, and they hold one advantage we cannot match: they are five minutes from your house, and a battery there is an errand rather than a plan. If that is the thing you value most, take it. We would say the same to our own family.

What tends to be missing is the second half of the transaction. Unbranded pieces sold with no guarantee and no papers are the ones that arrive at our bench with a stripped crown in month three, and the parts to repair them do not exist at any price. The question worth asking in any shop, in Elbasan or anywhere else, is who honours the guarantee and what happens in month seven. If the answer is a shrug, the price on the label was not the price.
