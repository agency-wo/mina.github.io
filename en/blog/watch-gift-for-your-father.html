A Watch for Your Father

He says he does not need anything, and he is not being polite. This is the one thing that gets worn anyway.

4 min read Iglisi Watch · Durrës August 2026

He Is Not Being Polite

When a father says he does not need anything he usually means it. He has bought what he wants for thirty years and the list is short. That is why most gifts for fathers end up as things he uses once to be kind about.

A watch survives that test because it is not decorative. It gets put on with everything else in the morning, and the ones that get worn are plain, steel and readable rather than interesting.

What Each Budget Buys

Prices from the shelf. Brand new, 1-year guarantee, brand box.

€50 to €65

A real watch that survives being worn every day. The Casio MTP-VD01 at €52 and the Daniel Klein Premium at €64 are the two that leave most often for a father who works.

€65 to €100

Where the case and the bracelet stop being an afterthought. The Navimarine Steel 5ATM Blue at €75 is the one we hand people who say the word father.

€150 and up

For a birthday that ends in a zero, or a retirement. The Hislon Classic at €199 has a sapphire crystal, which is the part that still looks new in ten years.

Look at What He Already Wears First

If there is a watch on his arm, copy its logic rather than replacing it. Steel stays steel. A man who has worn a bracelet for a decade will not start with leather because it arrived wrapped.

If there is a watch in his drawer instead, that is the more interesting conversation. Bring it in. A battery, a strap and a clean is often the whole problem, and a watch he already chose beats one you chose for him.

If you want one suggestion rather than a shelf, this is what we hand people buying for a father.

Frequently Asked Questions

He already has a watch he never takes off. What then?

Then the gift might be servicing it rather than replacing it. Bring it in and we will tell you what it needs and what it costs, and if the answer is a battery and a clean we will say that instead of selling you a new one. A watch he has worn for twenty years beats anything in a box.

How much should I spend on a watch for my father?

€60 to €80 covers most of it, and the step up in that range is real: a heavier case and a better bracelet, which are the two things he will notice daily. Above €150 you are buying a piece rather than a tool, and that is a different decision.

What kind of watch do fathers actually wear?

Steel, a dial readable without glasses, and a bracelet rather than leather if he works with his hands. Large plain numerals get worn more than anything with a busy dial, and that is not a compromise, it is the point.

Does it come with a guarantee, and can he change it?

Every watch is brand new with a 1-year guarantee and leaves in its brand box. Unworn within 14 days with the box and we change it, and he can come in on his own to do it. If only the bracelet is wrong we size it free at the counter while he waits.
