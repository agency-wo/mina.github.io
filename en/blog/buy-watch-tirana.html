Buying a Watch in Tirana: What Your Options Are

Import-taxed Swiss at one end, unbranded stalls at the other, and a family watchmaker 40 minutes down the highway in between. Free delivery to Tirana, cash at the door.

5 min read August 2026

Tirana has more watch shops than anywhere else in Albania, and buyers still send us messages from Tirana every week. This is what the capital actually offers at each end of the market, where we sit between them, and the two ways to buy from us without wasting a Saturday.

Two routes. The workshop in Durrës is about 40 minutes from Tirana on the highway with free parking outside the door, or the courier brings the watch to your address in normally 3 to 7 days for nothing. Payment is cash at the door either way, and the price is identical. See our delivery, payment and returns page for the full policy.

What Tirana Sells at the Two Ends of the Market

The top of the Tirana market is the imported Swiss and German marques in the boulevard boutiques and the shopping centres. If you want a Tissot, a Longines or a TAG Heuer, Tirana is genuinely where to go and we would tell you so. What you should expect is the price: those watches carry import duty wherever they are sold in Albania, and the entry point is several hundred euros before you have looked at anything with a complication.

The bottom of the market is the other extreme. Unbranded pieces on market stalls and in phone-accessory shops, sold with no guarantee, no papers and nobody to go back to when the crown pulls out in the third month. We see those on the bench often enough to know how they end, and the parts to repair them do not exist.

Where We Sit Between Them

Iglisi Watch is a family watchmaker in Durrës, open since 2009, and the counter runs from €50 to €249. Everything is brand new, everything carries our own 1-year guarantee, and the person who sells you the watch is the person who services it afterwards.

Hislon and Navimarine are the two brands on our counter that Tirana retailers do not carry, so the comparison you make in a shopping centre will not include them. That is worth knowing before you decide the range in the capital is the whole range in the country. Our side by side on Hislon and Navimarine sets out which suits which buyer.

The Two Ways to Buy from Tirana

Drive down. Durrës is about 40 minutes on the highway and the workshop is on Rruga Aleksander Goga, open Monday to Saturday from 8:30 to 20:30 with no appointment and free parking outside the door. Putting three watches on a counter under one light settles a decision faster than a week of photographs, and there is no obligation attached to looking.

Or do not. Send the model on WhatsApp, we confirm it is on the shelf, and a courier brings it to your Tirana address in normally 3 to 7 days. Delivery is free, there is no deposit and no card details, and you pay in cash once the box is open and you are happy with what is in it. Hand it back at the door and it has cost you nothing.

Not sure which model? The message we get most is a description of a person and a budget rather than a model name. Send us that instead and we will come back with two or three specific watches from current stock, which is faster than scrolling and far better than a courier making the trip twice.

Three Watches Tirana Customers Ask For

The Hislon Classic CL213S-02SS at 199 euro (18,400 L) is the one bought instead of an import-taxed dress watch: fluted bezel, horizontal-stripe silver dial, sapphire crystal and a date window. Sapphire is one of the details you are paying for at the top of a boutique window, and it is on this dial without the import duty.

The Navimarine NM181-03 at 70 euro (6,500 L) is the sportiest piece we stock: rotating blue bezel, navy dial, luminous round markers, a yellow seconds hand, steel case and bracelet, quartz movement and a date window. It is the answer to a market stall diver lookalike, from a brand with a warranty behind it.

The Philippe Lauren Steel Crystal Blue at 52 euro (4,800 L) is the gift that leaves most often for a Tirana address: a stainless steel ladies dress watch with a crystal-set bezel in two rows, a deep blue sunburst dial with crystal indices and a five-link polished bracelet. It is the dressiest Philippe Lauren on the counter.

Buying a Watch as a Gift for Someone in Tirana

This is one of the most common orders we handle, and it usually comes from Durrës or from abroad. The process does not change: pick the model, message us, and we deliver to the recipient rather than to you. Payment can be settled by the giver in advance so that nothing is asked of the person opening the box.

We leave the price out of the package if you ask, and we can add a note. Weddings, graduations and name days are the occasions we see most, and we are used to working backwards from a fixed date rather than from the day you ordered. Tell us the day on WhatsApp and we will book the courier around it.

What Happens After the Watch Is Yours

You have 30 days to bring it back to Durrës, unworn and in its box, for an exchange or a refund, and that applies to a courier delivery exactly as it does to a counter sale. The 1-year guarantee runs from the date of purchase on top of any manufacturer warranty.

A steel bracelet arrives long and we take links out free at the counter. If the courier brought it to Tirana rather than you collecting it, you are still welcome to walk in and have it sized, and the same goes for a battery or a strap in three years. That is the part a shopping centre display case does not come with, and it is most of the reason the 40 minutes is worth doing once.
