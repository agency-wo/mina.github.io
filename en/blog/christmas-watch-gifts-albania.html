Christmas Watch Gifts in Albania

What each budget actually buys, the last sensible day to order, and what to choose when you have no idea what they like.

4 min read Iglisi Watch · Durrës August 2026

Why a Watch Still Works at Christmas

Most Christmas presents are used once and then live in a drawer. A watch is put on the next morning and every morning after that, which is the whole reason it has survived as a gift while so much else has not. It is also the rare present that can be repaired instead of replaced. We have been on Rruga Aleksander Goga since 2009, and a watch bought here can be brought back here.

It helps in the other direction too. Nobody has to perform delight in front of the family, because a watch is not a taste test. Steel, a clean dial and a guarantee lands whether or not you got every detail right.

What Each Budget Buys

These are prices from the shelf, not a range invented for an article. Every watch is brand new and carries a 1-year guarantee.

€50 to €60

A real, brand-new watch from a name people recognise. The Casio MTP-VD01 at €52 is the one most people leave with at this level.

€60 to €75

Heavier cases and better finishing, and where most Christmas gifts land. The Daniel Klein Premium at €64 sits here, and so does the Romanson BH3054GBR at €74, which is the cheapest sapphire crystal we stock.

€75 to €100

Where a watch starts to feel like a piece rather than an accessory. The Navimarine NT0031-1 at €85 is the one people pick when they want it to look like it cost more than it did.

€150 and up

The Hislon Classic at €199 is the top of what we stock. It is what people buy for a Christmas that is also a birthday, a promotion or a first year of marriage.

The Last Sensible Day to Order

In Durrës there is no deadline. We are open Monday to Saturday until 20:30, including the week of Christmas. Walk in, choose, and leave with it boxed.

Anywhere else in Albania, message us by 18 December. It will reach you before the 25th, paid in cash to the courier. Later than that and we will still send it, but we will tell you first whether it is going to make it rather than let you find out on the 24th.

If you are cutting it fine, the honest answer is usually to come in. Our same-day gift guide covers what is possible on the day itself.

If You Do Not Know What They Like

This is the most common message we get in December, and the answer has not changed: steel case, plain dial, simple bracelet. It goes with everything, and it is the safest thing to give somebody whose wardrobe you have not studied.

Avoid anything loud unless you already know they wear loud things. A gold-tone watch on somebody who only wears steel is the present that gets admired once on the day and never appears again.

If you would rather have one suggestion than a catalogue, this is the one we hand people most often at Christmas.

Ordering From Outside Durrës

Send a WhatsApp with the model you want, or just a sentence about who it is for and roughly what you want to spend, and we will send back photographs of what fits. Delivery goes anywhere in Albania and you pay the courier in cash when it arrives, so no money leaves your hands before the watch reaches them.

There is no cart and no card form on this site, on purpose. A two-minute conversation gets a better gift than a filter does, because we can tell you what has been leaving the shop and what has been coming back.

After the 25th

If the bracelet needs adjusting we size it free at the counter, and that includes watches bought online: you are welcome to walk in with it. A battery change is the same, done while you wait rather than sent away.

If it is genuinely the wrong watch, bring it back unworn within 14 days with the box and we will change it. That is easier to say in December than to argue about in January, so it is worth saying now.
