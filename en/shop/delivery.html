Delivery, Payment and Returns

Free delivery anywhere in Albania, you pay the courier when the watch is in your hands, and you have 30 days to bring it back if it is not right. Here is exactly how ordering from Durrës works.

Buying a watch you have not held is a reasonable thing to hesitate about, so this page sets out every part of it plainly: how to order, what delivery costs, when you pay, what happens if it is wrong, and what the guarantee actually covers. No small print anywhere else contradicts this page.

How to Order

  1. Find the watch. The full stock is on the site with prices in euro and Lek, and everything shown is in the shop in Durrës.
  2. Send a WhatsApp. Message +355 67 636 0510 with the model name or reference. If you are not sure which one, tell us the budget and how it will be worn and we will send back two or three specific options rather than a catalogue.
  3. We confirm. We check the watch is in stock, confirm the price, and agree delivery or collection with you.
  4. It arrives. You inspect it, and you pay the courier.

Delivery

Free, anywhere in Albania. Tirana, Vlorë, Shkodër, Elbasan, Korçë, Fier, Berat, Lushnjë, Gjirokastër, Sarandë and everywhere in between. Delivery normally takes 3 to 7 days and runs through a courier we use regularly.

There is no markup for ordering from outside Durrës. The price you see is the price at the counter, whether you are ten minutes away or at the other end of the country. We do not add a delivery fee and we do not price differently by city.

If you would rather collect, or you are passing through Durrës, the workshop is on Rruga Aleksander Goga and we are open Monday to Saturday, 8:30 to 20:30. No appointment.

Payment

Cash on delivery. You pay the courier at the door, when the watch is physically in your hands. Nothing is paid in advance, there is no deposit, and we do not ask for card details.

That is deliberate. It means the risk of ordering something you have not seen sits with us rather than with you, which is the only fair way to sell a watch to someone two hundred kilometres away.

Inspect It Before You Pay

Open the box at the door and look at it properly. Check that it is the model you ordered, that the dial and bracelet are what you expected, and that it sits on your wrist the way you hoped. Bracelets are adjustable, so a link or two is not a reason to reject it, and we size them free at the counter afterwards.

If it is not right, hand it straight back to the courier without paying. That costs you nothing and there is no obligation of any kind. Message us afterwards and we will work out what would suit better.

Returns: 30 Days

If you have accepted and paid for a watch and then change your mind, you can bring it back to the shop in Durrës within 30 days, unworn and in its box, for an exchange or a refund. This applies to a watch delivered by courier exactly as it does to one bought at the counter.

Beyond those 30 days a return is at our discretion, other than in the cases every seller must honour: a watch proven counterfeit or materially misdescribed, and anything required by Albanian consumer protection law. The full wording is in our terms and conditions.

The 1-Year Guarantee

Every watch we sell is brand new and carries our own 1-year guarantee from the date of purchase, in addition to any manufacturer warranty. If the watch stops working under normal use within twelve months because of a manufacturing fault, we repair or replace it.

What it does not cover is the ordinary list: accidental damage, water damage beyond the rating printed on the dial, battery replacement, strap wear, and faults caused by someone else opening the case. A watch that stops after three years with no maintenance is not a warranty claim either, which is why the battery matters. There is more in our guide to what a watch warranty covers.

Afterwards: Batteries and Repairs

A quartz watch needs a battery roughly every couple of years, and we change one for 200 to 600 Lek depending on the battery, while you wait. Anything beyond that, a crystal, a strap, a full service, depends on the part and the movement, so we quote it before starting. No surprise charges, and we will tell you honestly when a repair is not worth doing.

That applies whether or not you bought the watch from us.

Still Unsure?

Message us. The commonest question we get is not about delivery at all, it is someone describing a person and a budget and asking what would suit. We would rather spend two minutes getting that right than have a watch travel to Shkodër and back.
