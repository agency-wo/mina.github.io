What You Cannot Find in Tirana That You Can Find Here

General jewellery shops and watch retailers in Tirana stock international brands at international prices, typically import-taxed Swiss and German marques that start at several hundred euros. The budget end of the Tirana market is dominated by low-quality Asian brands sold at street stalls and markets, with no guarantee and no after-sales support.

Navimarine and Hislon occupy a gap between those two worlds. Both are genuine watch brands with real quality control, real warranty programmes, and real after-sales support. Hislon is a Swiss brand with watches built to Swiss standards. Navimarine is a dedicated sports and everyday watch brand with solid stainless steel cases and bracelets across the range. Neither is sold in Tirana shops. Iglisi Watch is the Albanian stockist.

If you want a brand-new watch with a 1-year guarantee at a price between €50 and €199, that is not import-taxed to double its value, bought from a business that has been in operation since 2009 and will still be here when the warranty matters - there is currently no option in Tirana that competes with what we offer.
