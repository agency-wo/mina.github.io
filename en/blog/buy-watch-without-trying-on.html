Is It Safe to Buy a Watch Without Trying It On?

Most of our orders go to people who have never set foot in the workshop. Here is what actually happens: you pay the courier at the door, you can hand it straight back, and we size the bracelet free afterwards.

9 min read Iglisi Watch - Durrës May 2026

The Short Answer

Yes, and you do not need to work anything out beforehand. Every watch we deliver is paid for at the door, in cash, once it is physically in your hands. You open the box in front of the courier. If it is not what you expected, you hand it straight back and pay nothing. If you keep it, you have 30 days to bring it back to Durrës for an exchange or a refund, and a 1-year guarantee after that.

The part people do not expect: if the steel bracelet arrives with a link or two too many, we take them out at the counter free of charge. That applies to a watch you ordered online exactly as it does to one bought in the shop. You are welcome to walk in for it.

What Protects You When You Order Remotely

You Pay at the Door

Nothing is paid in advance. No deposit, no bank transfer, no card details. The courier takes the cash when the watch is in your hands, which puts the risk of sending something you have not seen on us rather than on you.

You Can Refuse It for Nothing

Open the box at the door and look at it properly. Check the model, the dial and the bracelet against what was agreed. If it is not right, hand it back to the courier without paying. That costs you nothing and there is no obligation of any kind.

Thirty Days to Change Your Mind

If you accept the watch and later think better of it, bring it back to the workshop within 30 days, unworn and in its box, for an exchange or a refund. A courier delivery carries exactly the same right as a purchase at the counter.

We Size the Bracelet Free

Steel bracelets ship long, and links come out in a few minutes on the bench. We do it at no charge, and we do it for online orders too. Come in whenever it suits you, Monday to Saturday, no appointment.

We Size the Bracelet Free, Even If You Ordered Online

This is the thing that makes a remote order a non-issue, and it is worth saying plainly. A steel bracelet is not a fixed object. It is a row of links held by pins, and taking links out is a routine bench job we do several times a day. Every watch bought at the counter gets it, and the owner has been clear that an online order gets it too: you are welcome to come in and have it done even if the watch arrived by courier.

It costs nothing, it takes a few minutes, and you do not need to book. The workshop is on Rruga Aleksander Goga in Durrës, open Monday to Saturday, 8:30 to 20:30. Bring the watch and the links we removed go back in the box with you, in case you ever want them again.

Buying as a gift? A steel bracelet is the easier choice precisely because it is adjustable afterwards. Bring the watch in with the person who received it and we will take the links out while you wait. If you would rather it went out on leather, say so before we send it and we will fit a strap at the counter first.

The Numbers, and How to Get Them

We do not print case diameters on the product pages, but every watch is in the building and we will measure any of them. Message us on WhatsApp with the model reference and we will send back the diameter, the thickness, the lug-to-lug and the strap width, along with a photograph taken that day if you want one.

Case thickness is the number that decides whether a watch slides under a shirt cuff. Anything under 9mm is genuinely slim, 10 to 11mm is standard for a steel watch with a date, and above 12mm the case has real depth to it. The Casio AE-1500WH is a resin sport case and sits well above that, which is part of the look rather than an accident.

Ask before you order. Send us the model reference on WhatsApp and we will measure the watch and photograph it the same day, with no obligation to buy. We would rather answer ten questions than send out a watch that comes straight back.

Lug-to-Lug and Strap Width

Lug-to-lug is the distance from the tip of the top lugs to the tip of the bottom lugs, measured in a straight line. It is the total length of the case, and it is not the same as the diameter: a 40mm case with long straight lugs can measure 48mm end to end, while a 42mm case with short curved lugs can measure 46mm. Brands publish it far less often than diameter, which is why we measure it on request.

Strap width is the gap between the lugs, and it is the number you will actually need later. A replacement strap has to match it exactly, so a 20mm watch takes a 20mm strap and nothing else. If you think you might swap a bracelet for leather at some point, ask us for the strap width when you order and write it down. We keep straps at the workshop and fit them while you wait.

How to Read Product Photos

Every watch on the site is photographed the same way: square, on a white background, under the same lighting. That is deliberate, and it makes the photos useful for comparison rather than for atmosphere. Two models opened in two browser tabs are directly comparable, because nothing about the setup changed between them.

Compare the case against its own bracelet in the same photograph. That proportion is real and it survives the photograph, so a case that looks broad against its bracelet is broad. What a white-background photograph cannot give you is absolute scale, which is exactly why we measure on request instead of asking you to judge it from an image.

We do not shoot watches from angles that make them look larger or more expensive than they are. A photograph that oversells a watch produces a refusal at the door, and a refusal at the door costs us the courier run and the sale.
