What Happens After You Order

We check the piece is on the shelf and send you a photograph of the actual unit if you ask for one. There is no deposit, no transfer and no card details, and there is no field anywhere on this site that would take a card number. Once you are happy we book the courier.

Delivery normally takes 3 to 7 days. The courier hands you the box, you open it and look at it properly, and you pay him in cash if you are satisfied. If you are not, hand it straight back without paying: that costs you nothing and there is no obligation of any kind. The mechanics of that are set out in full in our guide to cash on delivery in Albania.

Paying the courier is not the end of it either. You have 30 days to bring the watch back to the shop, unworn and in its box, for an exchange or a refund, and the 1-year guarantee runs from the date of purchase on top of any manufacturer warranty.
