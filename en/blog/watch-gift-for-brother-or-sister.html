A Watch for a Brother or a Sister

The one present they will wear in front of you every week, which is why it is the hardest easy gift there is.

4 min read Iglisi Watch · Durrës August 2026

Why a Sibling Is the Hardest Easy Gift

A watch is easy to buy for somebody you barely know. It is hard to buy for a sibling, because they will wear it in front of you, or not wear it in front of you, and both of those are a message. A colleague puts your gift in a drawer and you never find out. A brother does not have that option.

The good news is that you already know the answer. You have seen what is on their arm for twenty years. The mistake is not knowing too little about their taste, it is buying for the version of them you think they should be.

For a Brother

Steel, a dial he can read across a room, and a bracelet rather than leather if he works with his hands. The {navi} is the one that leaves most often for a brother who is on his feet all day.

If he already wears something, look at it before you buy. Somebody in a gold-tone watch does not want a steel one and the reverse is just as true. Matching what he already chose is not unimaginative, it is the whole trick.

For a Sister

The same logic and a different shelf. The {dk190} at €59 and the {dk165} at €75 are the two that move, and the difference between them is finish rather than function.

Watch what she actually wears rather than what is in the photographs. A watch that goes with everything gets worn on a Tuesday, and a watch that goes with one outfit gets worn at weddings.

If you want one suggestion instead of a shelf, this is the one we reach for most often when somebody says the word sibling.

If You Are Buying for Two

Two of the same watch is a statement and you should make it on purpose. The safer version is one brand in two cases, which reads as thought without turning into a uniform.

Buying two at once is also the one time it is worth asking us what is coming in. We hold more than the site shows, and a pair that works together is easier to find in the drawer than on a screen.

If You Get It Wrong

Unworn, within 14 days, with the box, and we change it. Nobody has to explain themselves and it is not a favour.

If the watch is right and only the bracelet is wrong, that is not getting it wrong. We size it free at the counter while you wait, including for watches bought online. Come in with it whenever suits.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I spend on a watch for a sibling?

Between €50 and €80 covers almost every case. A sibling gift that costs too much creates an obligation to match it next year, and that is a real thing in a family rather than a polite worry. Our guide on how much to spend goes through it properly.

Is it strange to give a brother and a sister the same watch?

Only if it is obviously the same watch. The same brand in two different cases reads as thought rather than as a two-for-one, and nobody has to be told the plan. Two of the identical model is a story they will tell about you for years, so do it deliberately or not at all.

What if they never wear a watch?

Then a watch is a bad gift and we would rather say so. Somebody who has not worn one in ten years does not start because it arrived in a box. The exception is a first job or a first flat, where the habit starts with the occasion rather than the object.

What if I get it wrong?

Bring it back unworn within 14 days with the box and we will change it. If the bracelet is the only problem we size it free at the counter, and that applies to watches bought online as well: you are welcome to come in with it.
