Mother's Day Watch Gifts in Albania

8 March, what each budget buys, and the difference between a gift that is admired on the day and one that is still on her arm in June.

4 min read Iglisi Watch · Durrës August 2026

8 March, and Why the Date Matters Here

Albania keeps Mother's Day on 8 March, alongside Women's Day, which means flowers are everywhere and nearly identical. That is the whole opportunity: a watch is the one gift on that day which is still there in June.

It also means the shops are busy. If you are in Durrës that is not your problem, because we are open until 20:30 and you can walk in. If you are ordering for somewhere else, the date below is the one that matters.

The Last Day to Order

In Durrës there is no deadline. Monday to Saturday until 20:30, including the week of 8 March. Walk in, choose, and leave with it boxed.

Anywhere else in Albania, message us by 1 March. It reaches you in time, paid in cash to the courier. Later than that and we will still send it, but we will tell you first whether it is going to arrive rather than let you find out on the 7th.

What Each Budget Buys

Prices from the shelf. Brand new, 1-year guarantee, brand box.

€55 to €62

Where most 8 March gifts sit. The Daniel Klein 14190 at €59, the Philippe Lauren PL2392-4 at €59 and the Daniel Klein F at €60 are all worn daily rather than kept for occasions.

€62 to €80

Better finishing, and the difference is visible across a table. The Daniel Klein 14165 at €75 is the one that leaves most often when somebody wants it to look like more than it cost.

Match the Colour She Already Wears

This is the one decision that decides whether it gets worn. Look at the rings and the bracelet already on her arm. If they are gold-tone, buy gold-tone. If they are silver or steel, buy steel. Nobody rearranges twenty years of jewellery around a present.

Everything else is smaller than it looks. Dial colour, bracelet or mesh, round or rectangular: these are preferences she can live with either way. Metal colour is the one she cannot.

If you want one suggestion rather than a shelf, this is what we hand people buying for 8 March.

Admired on the Day, or Worn All Year

The difference is usually plainness. A watch with one clean dial and a simple bracelet goes on with everything, and that is the one still being worn in June. Something with more going on gets admired properly on the day and then comes out for weddings.

Both are legitimate gifts. It is worth knowing which one you are buying before you buy it, rather than being surprised in the summer.

If It Is Not Right

Unworn within 14 days with the box and we change it, and she can do that herself without you standing there. Nothing has to be explained.

If the watch is right and the bracelet is loose, that is not a return. We size it free at the counter while she waits, including for watches bought online, and she is welcome to come in whenever suits her.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Mother's Day in Albania?

8 March, kept alongside Women's Day. If you are ordering for delivery anywhere outside Durrës, message us by 1 March and it will reach you in time. In Durrës there is no deadline at all: we are open Monday to Saturday until 20:30, including that week.

How much should I spend?

Between €55 and €80 is where almost every 8 March gift lands, and it buys a real watch with a 1-year guarantee rather than a token. Above that you are buying finish, which is worth it for a birthday that ends in a zero and not particularly otherwise.

How do I choose if I have never bought her jewellery?

Look at what is already on her arm and match the colour rather than the style. Somebody who wears gold-tone rings will not switch to steel because it arrived in a box. That single decision matters more than every other choice on this page.

What if it is the wrong one?

Unworn within 14 days with the box and we change it, and she can come in herself without you. If the bracelet is the only problem we size it free at the counter while she waits, and that includes watches bought online.
